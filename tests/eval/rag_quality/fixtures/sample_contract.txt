SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of 1st January 2025, by and between:

Client: Sharma & Associates Law LLP, a limited liability partnership incorporated under the laws of India, with its registered office at 14, Connaught Place, New Delhi - 110001 ("Client"), and

Service Provider: TechSolve India Pvt. Ltd., a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, with its registered office at 45, Koramangala, Bengaluru - 560034 ("Service Provider").

RECITALS

WHEREAS, the Client desires to engage the Service Provider to provide certain software development and IT consulting services as described herein; and

WHEREAS, the Service Provider has agreed to provide such services subject to the terms and conditions set forth in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements hereinafter set forth, the parties hereby agree as follows:

=== PAGE 2 ===

1. SCOPE OF SERVICES

1.1 The Service Provider agrees to provide the following services ("Services"):
    (a) Custom software development for the Client's internal case management system;
    (b) API integration with the Client's existing data infrastructure;
    (c) Technical support and maintenance for a period of twelve (12) months post-deployment;
    (d) Training sessions for up to twenty (20) Client employees.

1.2 The Services shall be performed in accordance with the Statement of Work ("SOW") attached hereto as Exhibit A, which is hereby incorporated by reference.

1.3 The Service Provider shall assign a dedicated Project Manager to coordinate all activities under this Agreement.

2. TERM

2.1 This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue for a period of twenty-four (24) months ("Initial Term"), unless earlier terminated in accordance with the provisions of this Agreement.

2.2 Upon expiry of the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive periods of twelve (12) months each ("Renewal Term"), unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days prior to the expiry of the then-current term.

=== PAGE 3 ===

3. COMPENSATION AND PAYMENT TERMS

3.1 In consideration for the Services, the Client agrees to pay the Service Provider a total contract value of Indian Rupees Forty-Five Lakhs (INR 45,00,000/-), exclusive of applicable taxes ("Contract Value").

3.2 Payment Schedule:
    (a) Milestone 1 – Contract Signing: INR 10,00,000/- (payable within 7 days of signing)
    (b) Milestone 2 – Prototype Delivery: INR 15,00,000/- (payable within 15 days of delivery and acceptance)
    (c) Milestone 3 – Final Deployment: INR 15,00,000/- (payable within 15 days of go-live)
    (d) Milestone 4 – Post-Deployment Support (Monthly): INR 41,667/- per month for 12 months

3.3 All payments shall be made by NEFT/RTGS to the Service Provider's designated bank account. Any delay in payment beyond the due date shall attract interest at the rate of eighteen percent (18%) per annum on the overdue amount.

3.4 In the event of a genuine dispute regarding an invoice, the Client shall notify the Service Provider within ten (10) business days of receipt. The undisputed portion of any invoice shall remain payable on the original due date.

4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

4.1 All work product, deliverables, source code, documentation, and other materials created specifically for the Client under this Agreement ("Work Product") shall, upon full payment of all amounts due hereunder, become the exclusive property of the Client.

4.2 The Service Provider hereby assigns to the Client all rights, title, and interest in and to the Work Product, including all intellectual property rights therein.

4.3 The Service Provider retains ownership of all pre-existing tools, frameworks, libraries, and methodologies ("Pre-Existing IP") used in the development of the Work Product. The Service Provider grants the Client a non-exclusive, irrevocable, royalty-free license to use the Pre-Existing IP solely to the extent embedded in and necessary for the use of the Work Product.

4.4 The Client shall retain exclusive ownership of all data, documents, and information provided by the Client to the Service Provider for the purposes of this Agreement ("Client Data").

5. CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 Each party agrees to keep confidential all non-public information disclosed by the other party in connection with this Agreement ("Confidential Information"), including but not limited to technical specifications, business strategies, client lists, pricing, and trade secrets.

5.2 Each party shall: (a) use the Confidential Information solely for the purposes of this Agreement; (b) restrict access to the Confidential Information to those of its employees and contractors who need to know such information for the purposes of this Agreement; and (c) protect the Confidential Information with at least the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information, but in no event less than reasonable care.

5.3 The obligations of confidentiality shall survive the termination or expiry of this Agreement for a period of five (5) years.

5.4 The Confidentiality obligations shall not apply to information that: (a) is or becomes publicly known through no breach of this Agreement; (b) was rightfully known to the receiving party prior to disclosure; (c) is rightfully received from a third party without restriction; or (d) is required to be disclosed by applicable law or court order, provided the disclosing party is given prompt written notice.

6. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

6.1 IN NO EVENT SHALL EITHER PARTY BE LIABLE TO THE OTHER FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES ARISING OUT OF OR RELATED TO THIS AGREEMENT, EVEN IF SUCH PARTY HAS BEEN ADVISED OF THE POSSIBILITY OF SUCH DAMAGES.

6.2 The aggregate liability of the Service Provider under this Agreement for any and all claims shall not exceed the total amount paid by the Client to the Service Provider in the twelve (12) months immediately preceding the claim giving rise to such liability.

6.3 The limitation of liability set forth in this Section shall not apply to: (a) breaches of confidentiality obligations; (b) indemnification obligations; (c) a party's fraud, gross negligence, or wilful misconduct; or (d) any liability that cannot be limited by applicable law.

7. TERMINATION

7.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement for cause upon thirty (30) days' written notice if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within the notice period.

7.2 The Client may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon sixty (60) days' written notice to the Service Provider.

7.3 Upon termination for convenience by the Client, the Client shall pay the Service Provider for all Services satisfactorily performed up to the date of termination, plus a termination fee equal to twenty percent (20%) of the remaining unpaid Contract Value.
